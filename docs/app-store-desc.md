## App Store Description（Full Version）

**Release intense emotions. Then leave.**

This app is for moments when emotions feel loud or overwhelming.
Not for therapy. Not for tracking. Not for fixing yourself.

Just a quiet place to let it out — and move on.

### How it works

Choose what you’re feeling.
Hold to release.
When it’s done, you’re done.

There’s nothing to analyze.
Nothing to understand.
Nothing to improve.

You don’t need the right words.
You don’t need to explain anything.

---

### What this app is — and isn’t

• No accounts
• No social features
• No streaks or reminders
• No advice or interpretation

This app doesn’t tell you how to feel.
It doesn’t ask you to reflect.
It doesn’t ask you to stay.

You can leave at any time.

---

### Privacy

Your release stays private.

Entries are stored locally on your device.
If analytics are enabled, only anonymous usage events are collected — never your notes or personal information.
