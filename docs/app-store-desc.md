# US Store Listing Copy

This document contains release-ready listing text for the U.S. App Store and Google Play.

## Product Name

Emotion Release

## Tagline (App Store)

Let it out, then move on.

## Short Description (Google Play, <= 80 chars)

A private space to release intense emotions and move on.

## Full Description

Release intense emotions in the moment.
No analysis. No judgment. Just release, acknowledge, and exit.

Emotion Release is designed for moments when feelings are loud, heavy, or overwhelming.
You choose what you feel, hold to release, and finish with a clear sense of completion.

### How it works

- Choose an emotion.
- Press and hold to release.
- Complete the flow and leave when you are ready.

### What this app is

- Private and low commitment
- Focused on immediate emotional release
- Built around body-first interaction

### What this app is not

- Not social
- Not a streak app
- Not therapy or medical treatment
- Not emotional analysis or advice

### Privacy

Your release data stays on your device.
If analytics are enabled, only anonymous product usage events are collected.
No personal notes or sensitive emotional content are sent as analytics payloads.

### Safety note

This app does not provide medical or crisis support.
If you are in the United States and need immediate help, call or text 988.
